# City of Sydney — Pensioner Rates Rebate

Eligible pensioners who own and occupy their home in the City of Sydney can receive a rebate of up to $250 off their annual council rates and domestic waste charges.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Own and occupy your home in the City of Sydney local government area as your sole or principal place of residence
- Hold a current Pensioner Concession Card issued by Centrelink or the Department of Veterans' Affairs

## How much

Up to $250 per year off ordinary rates and domestic waste charges. The NSW Government funds 55% of this rebate; the council funds 45%.

## How to apply

Download the application form from the City of Sydney website, complete it, and submit with a copy of your concession card. You only need to apply once — the rebate continues each year automatically unless your circumstances change.
