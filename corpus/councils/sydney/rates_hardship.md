# City of Sydney — Rates Hardship Assistance

The City of Sydney Council provides financial assistance for ratepayers experiencing genuine financial hardship who are struggling to pay their council rates.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Own and occupy a property in the City of Sydney local government area
- Are experiencing genuine financial hardship that makes it difficult to pay your rates on time

No concession card is required. There is no income threshold.

## What assistance is available

- Flexible payment plans (pay in instalments)
- Waiver or deferral of interest charges on overdue rates
- Formal hardship agreements for extended deferral

## How to apply

Contact the City of Sydney Council rates team directly to discuss your situation. You will need to explain your circumstances — the council assesses each case individually.
