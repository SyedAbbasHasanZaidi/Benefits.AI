# City of Sydney — Aquatic Centre Access Card

The City of Sydney Aquatic + Fitness Centre Access Card provides heavily discounted entry to the council's aquatic and fitness centres for eligible concession card holders.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Live in the City of Sydney local government area
- Hold one of the following cards:
  - Pensioner Concession Card
  - Health Care Card (including Low Income Health Care Card)
  - Commonwealth Seniors Health Card

## Discounted rates

- **Swimming:** $2 per visit (standard adult entry is $8.50)
- **Gym access:** $5.50 per visit

Applies at all City of Sydney aquatic centres including Ian Thorpe Aquatic Centre, Prince Alfred Park Pool, Cook + Phillip Park Aquatic + Fitness Centre, and others.

## How to apply

Apply online at the City of Sydney website or in person at any City of Sydney aquatic centre. Bring your concession card as proof of eligibility.
