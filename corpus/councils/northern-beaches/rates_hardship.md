# Northern Beaches Council — Rates Hardship Assistance

Northern Beaches Council provides assistance for ratepayers experiencing genuine financial hardship who cannot pay their council rates.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Own and occupy a property in the Northern Beaches Council area
- Are experiencing genuine financial hardship

## What assistance is available

- Flexible payment arrangements
- Interest relief on overdue accounts
- Hardship policy assessment for extended deferral

## How to apply

Contact Northern Beaches Council directly to discuss your situation and request hardship assistance.
