# Northern Beaches Council — Pensioner Rates Rebate

Eligible pensioners who own and occupy their home in the Northern Beaches Council area can receive a rebate off their annual council rates.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Own and occupy your home in the Northern Beaches Council local government area as your sole or principal place of residence
- Hold a current Pensioner Concession Card issued by Centrelink or the Department of Veterans' Affairs

## How much

Up to $250 per year off ordinary rates and domestic waste charges, in accordance with the NSW Pensioners' Rates and Annual Charges Concession Policy.

## How to apply

Complete the pensioner concession application form available from Northern Beaches Council. You only need to apply once.
