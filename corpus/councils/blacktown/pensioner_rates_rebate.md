# Blacktown City Council — Pensioner Rates Rebate

Eligible pensioners who own and occupy their home in the Blacktown City Council area can receive a rebate of up to $250 off their annual council rates.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Own and occupy your home in the Blacktown City Council local government area as your sole or principal place of residence
- Hold a current Pensioner Concession Card issued by Centrelink or the Department of Veterans' Affairs

## How much

Up to $250 per year off ordinary rates and domestic waste charges.

## How to apply

Download the application form from Blacktown City Council's website, complete it, and submit with a copy of your concession card.
