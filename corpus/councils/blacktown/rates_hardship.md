# Blacktown City Council — Rates Hardship Assistance

Blacktown City Council provides financial assistance for ratepayers experiencing genuine financial hardship who cannot pay their council rates.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Own and occupy a property in the Blacktown City Council area
- Are experiencing genuine financial hardship

No concession card or income test required.

## What assistance is available

- Flexible payment arrangements
- Deferral of rates
- Interest relief on overdue balances

## How to apply

Contact Blacktown City Council directly to discuss your situation and request hardship assistance.
