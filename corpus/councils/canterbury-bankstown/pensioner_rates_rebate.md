# Canterbury-Bankstown Council — Pensioner Rates Rebate

Eligible pensioners who own and occupy their home in the City of Canterbury Bankstown area can receive a rebate off their annual council rates.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Own and occupy your home in the Canterbury-Bankstown local government area as your sole or principal place of residence
- Hold a current Pensioner Concession Card issued by Centrelink or the Department of Veterans' Affairs

## How much

Up to $250 per year off ordinary rates and domestic waste charges.

## How to apply

Complete the Application for Council Pensioner Concession Rates Rebate form available on the City of Canterbury Bankstown website and submit with your concession card.
