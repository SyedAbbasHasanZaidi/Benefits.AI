# Canterbury-Bankstown Council — Rates Hardship Assistance

City of Canterbury Bankstown provides hardship assistance for ratepayers who are struggling to pay their rates, including a long-term deferral option for pensioners.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Own and occupy a property in the Canterbury-Bankstown local government area
- Are experiencing financial hardship

## What assistance is available

- Payment plans
- Interest waivers
- **Pensioner deferral agreement:** eligible pensioners can defer their rates for up to 19 years, with the balance repayable when the property is sold

## How to apply

Contact City of Canterbury Bankstown or complete the hardship application form on their website.
