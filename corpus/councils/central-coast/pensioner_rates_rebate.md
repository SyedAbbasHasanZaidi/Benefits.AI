# Central Coast Council — Pensioner Rates Rebate

Eligible pensioners who own and occupy their home in the Central Coast Council area can receive a rebate on their annual rates and domestic waste charges.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Own and occupy your home in the Central Coast Council local government area as your sole or principal place of residence
- Hold a current Pensioner Concession Card issued by Centrelink or the Department of Veterans' Affairs

## How much

Up to $250 per year off rates and domestic waste charges. Central Coast Council also provides an additional water rebate of up to $175 per year (up to $43.75 per quarter) for eligible pensioners where council provides water services.

## How to apply

Complete the Application for Council Pensioner Concession Rates and Charges Rebate form available on the Central Coast Council website.
