# Central Coast Council — Rates Hardship Assistance

Central Coast Council provides assistance for ratepayers experiencing financial hardship who cannot pay their rates or water bills on time.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:
- Own and occupy a property in the Central Coast Council area
- Are experiencing genuine financial hardship

## What assistance is available

- Alternative payment plans
- Deferral of rates or water charges
- Interest waivers on overdue accounts

## How to apply

Contact Central Coast Council directly or visit their website to discuss hardship assistance options.
